> This Google Maps listing is **NOT a typical private civilian dental office owned by an independent dentist**.  
> It is the **Benjamin W. Dunn USAF Dental Clinic**, part of the **59th Dental Group / 59th Medical Wing** of the **United States Air Force** located inside **Joint Base San Antonio (JBSA) – Lackland, Texas**.

### Why This Matters for Cold Outreach:
1. **Ownership:** It is a federal military healthcare treatment facility and dental residency training center (AEGD — Advanced Education in General Dentistry), named after Brigadier General (Dr.) Benjamin W. Dunn.
